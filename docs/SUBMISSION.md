## Prepared demo route

1. Use an owned/permissioned recording with a specific real setup, answer/turn and consequence. Retain its licence and human reference transcript. Supplied YouTube links are test references, not redistribution licences.
2. Choose source/output language before import. Queue discovery; show actual counts and partial results while editing.
3. Open a source-linked candidate. Keep the question and answer together; show the evidence, Moment Map and conservative rejection of weak material.
4. Change subtitle language; compare it with the original. Correct a name/timing and resolve a review flag. Demonstrate confirmed versus unconfirmed Story Memory.
5. Trim/split/reframe, queue an MP4, preview its sound/subtitles and download it. Show refresh/restart recovery with no silent repeated model call.
6. Present the real Arabic smoke-test error and the next native-review gate honestly. Do not describe the synthetic rendering fixture as an actual reality-show success.

Public code is MIT; model APIs, footage, fonts and referenced datasets have their own terms. No private competitor implementation was copied. No virality guarantee, emotion diagnosis, universal dialect claim or live reliability claim should appear in the pitch.
