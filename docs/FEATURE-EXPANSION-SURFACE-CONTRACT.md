# Context Desk feature expansion: surface contract

Direction owner: this implementation, continuing the existing peach/charcoal/Manrope design system. This is a code-led extension, not a new 12ui-generated or user-approved composition. The user requested the full competitor feature vision and rejected the previous UI as basic and confusing.

The supplied screenshots foreground footage, editable captions and a timeline. The illustrative Context Desk screen makes the Moment Map and source-linked stories the identity. ## Surfaces and hierarchy

- Home is the default entry, with a large two-line Manrope heading, one link field and Find clips action, secondary upload/live actions, one actual current-project image and a saved-project list. No invented examples, counters or virality claims.
- The six destinations have stable hash URLs: Home, Projects, Discover, Studio, Jobs and New video. Browser Back/Forward works. Same-Studio navigation must preserve playback.
- Studio reserves the center for actual video and trimming, the left for clip switching, and the right for one selected task: Captions, Style, Story or Export. Narrow screens put clips in a horizontal rail and stack the inspector after the viewer.
- Expensive/advanced tools are explicit: deep discovery preview, natural-language search, generated artwork, encoder setup and publishing. Selecting a style or opening a page must not call a paid model.
- Jobs distinguishes source processing, rendered videos, artwork and ZIP packaging; progress reflects completed work. Completed media remains playable and downloadable.
- Trashed projects remain visibly in Trash until the user restores them. Home must not imply their retained media was lost or that no saved projects exist.

## Durable design contracts

Self-hosted Manrope; display at most 58px desktop, 36px mobile; body minimum 14px in general content, functional metadata minimum 12px. Peach #ffb283 on near-black surfaces continues the committed brand. Typography, source imagery and the Moment Map carry the design; no gradients, glow, icon-card hero or fake growth scores.

All text/controls require sufficient contrast, 44px primary targets, visible focus, stable labels, loading/error/empty states, and no horizontal page overflow at 390, 484, 768 and 1440px. Inspector internals may scroll vertically; the footage must stay usable. Advanced functions should use disclosures rather than another wall of permanently open controls.

## Acceptance evidence

Review Home, populated Discover, all Studio inspector states, saved Projects, Jobs and Settings/Connections at desktop and narrow sizes. Exercise real video preview across volume/clip/tab changes, subtitle output policy, saved brand styles, transcript cuts with undo, no-face reframing fallback, actual XML/ZIP/MP4 outputs, explicit Meta request boundaries and missing-account setup. Capability claims in documentation must separate local/native/provider/fake-transport and externally connected tests.
