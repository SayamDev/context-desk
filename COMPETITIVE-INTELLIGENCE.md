# Context Desk V2 competitive intelligence

Research date: 1 November 2026. Scope: public product pages, first-party help, and normal inspection of public OpenShorts source. This is a product/design decision record, not a benchmark of clipping accuracy or a competitor-parity commitment.

## Evidence and access boundaries

- **P — public page:** text and controls exposed by an unauthenticated product page. This establishes what the page presents, not that its editor or backend works.
- **D — documented:** a first-party guide describes behavior. It has not been exercised in an authenticated competitor project.
- **M — marketing:** performance, quality, virality, and “understanding” claims made by the vendor; unverified here.
- **S — source:** behavior or licence visible in a public source file. Static inspection is not execution, a dependency audit, or proof of a deployed version.
- **I — inference/proposal:** our interpretation or suggested Context Desk implementation, explicitly distinct from competitor behavior.

No competitor account was created or entered, no payment or model job was started, and no authentication, paywall, or access restriction was bypassed. No proprietary code, templates, artwork, or bundled demo footage was copied. This research used web text extraction; it does **not** establish pixel-level editor observations. Actual browser screenshots belong in the separate browser evidence record and must identify the captured public URL and visible state. Never relabel a promotional editor image as an editor we operated.

Access limits: the guessed `www.vidiq.com/ai-clips/` and `storysonic.com/guide` URLs could not be opened by the text tool; the official vidIQ help and Storysonic homepage were accessible. No conclusion about product availability follows from these tool failures. Prices and plan limits can change; they are friction signals rather than a purchasing recommendation.

**Interpretation:** candidate generation is common category behavior, not the differentiator. The opportunity is to reduce the distance between an uncertain suggestion and a watchable, editable, source-verifiable cut. These sources do not establish that competitors lack provenance or context tools; V2 should demonstrate its own behavior instead of making an absence claim.

**Design conclusion — I:** premium here means a large reliable video surface, readable time, predictable selection, responsive controls and restrained information density. A miniature pro-editor skin with decorative tracks and nonfunctional buttons would not meet that bar. The user-authorized V2 direction replaces the previous plum/lilac/lime palette with charcoal surfaces and warm peach signal accents, keeping the video the brightest surface.

## OpenShorts licence and architecture inspection

The [root MIT licence](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mutonby/openshorts/main/LICENSE) permits reuse subject to its notice requirements and disclaims warranties. However, [NOTICE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mutonby/openshorts/main/NOTICE) explicitly excludes `cloud/` from MIT. Its [commercial licence](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mutonby/openshorts/main/cloud/LICENSE) permits limited personal/internal use and restricts third-party hosted/paid service use without a separate agreement. This is a material scope distinction: **do not describe the whole repository as MIT**. Any later reuse would require file- and dependency-specific review; no reuse was made for this research.

Static source observations, inspected on the public `main` branch on the research date (moving references, not a pinned release):

- [clip_selection.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mutonby/openshorts/main/clip_selection.py) contains transcript windows aligned to segments, balanced scoring batches, overlap deduplication and clip-boundary adjustment against word timestamps. These are implemented helpers, not proof of ranking accuracy or production outcomes. Do not repeat comments containing internal-looking retention statistics as independently verified findings.
- [edit_builder.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mutonby/openshorts/main/edit_builder.py) validates a constrained edit list and constructs FFmpeg filters, with strength/duration bounds and caption-sensitive zoom restrictions. This is useful architectural evidence for separating model proposals from deterministic rendering. Context Desk should implement its own bounded contract, not accept arbitrary generated render commands.
- [dashboard/package.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mutonby/openshorts/main/dashboard/package.json) lists React/Vite, Remotion-related packages, Mediabunny and Zod. A manifest proves declared dependencies, not which path runs for a given clip, licence compatibility across the full system, or deployed performance.

**Architecture inference — I:** useful common boundaries are ingest → source metadata/ASR → candidate proposals → validated edit decisions → synchronized preview → render/export. Vendor frontend pages cannot reveal server queues, model routing, storage retention, failure recovery, or real per-job cost. The inspected public source does not establish any closed-source competitor’s architecture.

## P0 acceptance path

1. Open a rights-cleared local video and reach a real preview with source identity and duration; show a useful empty state before import.
2. Select a real candidate or mark a range manually. Scrub, play/pause, restart and loop it; select/trim with keyboard and numeric alternatives.
3. Verify source context; correct captions and framing with immediate visual feedback. Preserve draft and uncertainty labels.
4. Export the chosen cut and SRT; inspect playback, audio, edge timing, dimensions and caption consistency. Record codec/browser limits instead of silently changing guarantees.
5. Exercise cancellation, unsupported input, source replacement and export failure. Show recoverable states and keep valid edits.

No numeric “virality” score is required. Any future ranking should name its basis, uncertainty and evaluation method; predicted engagement cannot guarantee distribution. Do not invent transcript, results, waveforms, thumbnails or progress to fill the workspace. A clean manual cut with transparent analysis limits is a stronger demo than an unverified automation claim.

## Research limits and follow-up evidence

This record supports a scoped V2 direction. It does not validate competitor quality, Arabic/dialect accuracy, visual reasoning, latency, privacy enforcement, public API reliability, or a production-ready Context Desk implementation. Competitor documentation can change and screenshots can lag releases. A later benchmark needs the same rights-cleared source set, explicit task criteria, measured cost/time, human correctness review and permission for any paid service. Until then use “documented,” “advertised,” and “proposed” accurately.
