# Context Desk product contract

**Audience:** a solo streamer or unscripted-show producer who needs the same simple default: frame source, scan, review, cut, export.

**Distinctive job:** turn a real live exchange into an editable, evidence-backed story clip while the source is still playing. Each suggestion keeps source time, transcript, a direct evidence note and an uncertainty note. Editorial controls remain one click away; detailed reframe, subtitle and export controls appear only when needed.

**Current workflow:** clean cropped tab/file capture → 12-second Meta speech and visual analysis → optional draft translation → moment editing → three-window story proposal → live cut preview → 720p/1080p local video, SRT, and JSON notes.

**Editorial rules:** no fabricated dialogue, reactions, identities, relationships, conflict, chronology or virality scores. Speech and translations are drafts. Captioned exports require human source-audio confirmation. Suggestions do not publish themselves.

**Current boundaries:** local session memory, browser codec and share permissions, turn-level (not word-level) ASR, no model-certified dialect accuracy, no complete Premiere/Resolve replacement. The core advantage to demonstrate is live, truth-preserving narrative editing, not a claim to have every feature of a mature nonlinear editor.
