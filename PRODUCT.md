# Context Desk product contract

**Audience:** a solo streamer or unscripted/reality-show producer, with one simple default: add a source, find moments, review context, edit and export.

**Distinctive job:** turn a permissioned recording or user-shared live feed into editable, source-linked story clips. Suggestions retain source times, the audio transcript, direct evidence and uncertainty. A real Moment Map exposes checked intervals and unreviewed gaps; overlapping discovery windows remain independently inspectable. Source review, caption correction and human editorial decisions take precedence over engagement claims.

**Current recording workflow:** paste a supported public URL or choose a local file; choose speech/output language and optional dialect, code-switch, name or glossary guidance; import into stable local storage; run bounded distributed discovery with Meta Muse Spark and source-audio transcription; review retained windows; trim/reframe, correct caption wording/timing and optional translation; queue a real FFmpeg video/SRT export; preview/download it in Jobs. Whole-recording subtitle jobs are separate from sampled moment discovery. Search operates on retained dialogue and evidence, not unseen footage.

**Current live workflow:** browser permission and tab audio → attempted automatic player framing with manual override → 24-second captured windows → source-audio transcription and sampled visual/story assessment → optional translation → independent window editing and optional multi-window sequence. Captured media is saved before analysis. Live capture and sustained background playback still require real-stream evaluation. Future footage cannot be captured once the original source ends; a browser crash can lose the current unfinalized window.

**Persistence:** SQLite owns project revisions, stable source/media assets, edits, completed outputs, provider caches and checkpointed background jobs. Library reopens saved work after refresh/restart; conflicting tabs preserve recovery copies; missing sources have an explicit relink path. Queued/unknown provider work becomes interrupted after restart and requires explicit retry. Undo/redo history is session-local; committed edits and independent split trims persist. This is a local single-computer app, not hosted cloud collaboration.

**Editorial rules:** no fabricated dialogue, reactions, identities, relationships, conflict, chronology, emotion diagnoses or virality scores. A target clip count is not guaranteed yield. Rejected analysis windows stay available and do not inflate Library's useful-clip count. Captioned exports require human source-audio confirmation and resolved uncertainty. Suggestions do not publish themselves. Suspense should preserve the actual setup, exchange and consequence.

**Language:** Meta Muse Voice supports the documented named-language hints; regional notes are guidance, not dialect classification or proof of accuracy. Muse turns do not have native word timestamps, so short-line timing is estimated. VoiceStudio may provide local transcription/translation and native word timings where available; its capability limits stay explicit. The app transcribes source audio rather than importing YouTube captions. Original ASR and translated edits stay distinct. One clean Arabic read-speech smoke test is not a dialect-rich accuracy benchmark.

**Current limits:** six-hour recording limit; local files up to 20 GB; direct-link working media budget of 4 GB with compatible audio and quality selection up to 1080p. Export source-size height is capped at 2160p, with 720p/1080p edited presets; upscaling does not restore detail. Automatic moving-subject/SAM tracking, full multitrack NLE controls, dubbing, scheduling, multicamera, production collaboration and universal platform ingestion are not implemented. Whole-episode editorial yield, native dialect/translation quality, sustained live capture and maximum-size stress remain validation gates. See STATUS.md and docs/V2-VERIFICATION.md.

**Design:** the V2 user-directed 12ui exploration replaces the earlier lilac/plum/lime world with a restrained charcoal/peach production suite. Video and real source time drive the interface. Impeccable supplies craft review; no Impeccable concept seed/roll was run or claimed. See DESIGN.md, UX-CONTRACT.md and docs/V2-VISUAL-RESEARCH.md.
